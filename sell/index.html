Questions

What people ask before they call

How fast can you actually close?
We can present an offer within 24 hours and close in as little as 10 days through a local title company — or later if you need time to move. You choose the date.
Do I have to clean or repair anything?
No. We buy completely as-is. Leave what you don't want to take; we'll handle it.
Are there fees or commissions?
No agent commissions and no listing fees. Standard closing costs go through the title company, and we walk you through every line before you sign.
Is the offer really no-obligation?
Yes. If the number doesn't work for your situation, we'll tell you straight whether listing with an agent would net you more. We'd rather be useful than pushy.
What if the house has tenants or code violations?
Still fine. We buy occupied properties, houses with liens, code issues, or open permits — those just get factored into the offer.
